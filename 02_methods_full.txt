RERUN AND CURATION PASS, 2026-11-12 (supplement to ../methods_and_limitations.txt of 2026-11-09)

1. Rerun. The three pairs were recomputed from scratch on the same laptop (macOS 13, 4 cores, 8 GB) with the same code, parameters and query set as on 2026-11-09 (scripts/run_pair.sh -> dl_genome.sh, run_miniprot_chunked.py, analyze_genes.py, compare_regions.py, finalize.py, run_sens_windows.py). Genome FASTA files were downloaded again from NCBI Datasets (same accessions) and deleted after the curation step. Differences from the original environment: samtools is no longer installed, so `samtools faidx` is served by bin/samtools, a wrapper around `seqkit faidx` (seqkit 2.12.0; identical .fai format and `>name:start-end` headers). miniprot 0.18-r281, minimap2 2.31-r1302, Python 3.14.8 as before. Reproducibility was checked by byte-wise comparison of family_summary, genes_per_haplotype, haplotype_differences and region_summary (main tables) and of the pairNsens tables (sensitive pass) against the 2026-11-09 files.

2. Curation pass (scripts/curate.py; one run per pair while the genomes were on disk; outputs in <pair>/curation/). It is automated, but it performs the checks that the 2026-11-09 limitations list as missing. Thresholds for supported/intact/pseudogene-like/partial are those of analyze_genes.py.
 2a. Locus re-alignment. Every locus (+-5 kb) was extracted and searched again with all 99 queries at the default k-mer settings (miniprot -j2 -p 0.3 -N 1000 --outs=0.3 --outc=0.3 --outn=1000 --gff --trans; -N/--outn are higher than in the pilot because the pilot's cap of 100 secondary alignments per query applied separately to each of 8-10 genome chunks, whereas here all ~100 regions of a haplotype are searched in one run). The first curation run of pair 1 used -N 100 --outn=200; pair 1 was re-curated with the raised caps after pairs 2 and 3 and gave identical tables (pair1/curation_first_run_1700 keeps the first run), so the caps are not what makes some loci disappear on re-alignment (see section 5). The status of the locus family on this small region was compared with the pilot status (column realign_consistent). The hit of the evidence query (or the best hit of the family) supplied the predicted protein (##STA line of miniprot --trans) and the CDS coordinates for the gene-model checks.
 2b. Gene-model checks (only possible from a genome: the pilot did not do them). start_codon: first codon of the spliced CDS when the alignment starts at query residue 1 (ATG / non-ATG(xxx) / not_aligned(q_start=N)); splice sites: donor and acceptor dinucleotides of every intron from the miniprot cs tag, canonical = GT-AG, GC-AG, AT-AC; terminal stop: last codon of the spliced CDS when a stop_codon feature is present; internal stops: translation of the spliced CDS (standard code) for frameshift-free hits. curated_intact = pilot intact AND re-aligned as intact AND ATG AND no non-canonical splice site AND terminal stop AND no internal stop AND CDS length divisible by 3. No signal-peptide or expression check.
 2c. Back-search (family verification). Predicted proteins were searched with diamond blastp 2.1.16 (--sensitive, -k 5) against UniProt Swiss-Prot reviewed entries of human (20,429) and mouse (17,285), release 2026_04, downloaded 2026-11-12 (ref/). The GN= gene name of the best hit was mapped to the pilot families with strict patterns (CD14 is not CD1, HLA-D*/H2-A,E,DM,O are class II, etc.). family_check: confirmed = best hit in the pilot family; ambiguous = best hit elsewhere but a hit of the pilot family within 90% of the top bit score; relabel:<family>(<gene>) otherwise. Paralog identity: all-vs-all diamond of the predicted proteins; for each locus the highest percent identity to another locus of the same family in the same haplotype (alignment >= 50% of the shorter protein).
 2d. Haplotype differences, locus level. Each locus reported as present in one haplotype only was extracted with 10 kb flanks and mapped to the WHOLE other haplotype assembly with minimap2 -x asm20 -c --cs -N 20 -p 0.5 -I 300M (multi-part index). Reported: fraction of the locus span aligned (best record), identity of that record, coverage of the left and right flank (union of all records on that contig), number of records covering >= 50% of the locus (multi_copy_landing), and whether the landing is inside the paired window of the pilot, on the same contig outside it, or on another contig. The landing region (+-10 kb) was searched with miniprot at default and at high sensitivity (-k5 -M0 -n2 -l4), the source region at high sensitivity; the family status was called on each with the pilot rules. For loci present in both haplotypes with different calls, both loci were re-searched at high sensitivity. When statuses still differ, the region of the lower-ranked copy was aligned to the region of the better copy (minimap2 -x asm5 -c --cs) and every variant inside the CDS exons of the better copy was listed: indels not divisible by 3 (frameshift; homopolymer run length >= 5 flagged, because such indels are the typical HiFi/assembly error), stop-gain substitutions (codon-aware), in-frame indels, and variants within 2 bp of an exon boundary (splice site). Classes: same_status_at_sensitive_realignment (a model of the same family and status was found in the investigated region); status_differs_confirmed (+ variants); locus_sequence_absent_from_<hap>_between_aligned_flanks (both flanks align, the locus does not); sequence_aligned_but_no_gene_hit; flags for N-runs and contig ends within 50 kb. In the summary (scripts/report_rerun.py) status_differs rows are further split into: with a disruptive variant (frameshift <= 50 bp, stop gain, splice site), with a large indel (> 50 bp) inside the gene, and without any candidate sequence change found in the examined alignment. Coverage and stop-feature thresholds remain possible explanations.
 2e. Gaps. Every >= 5 kb haplotype-specific gap or indel of the pilot (deduplicated: the pilot lists one event once per window pair that contains it) was mapped to the whole other haplotype in the same minimap2 run and classified: present_in_paired_window (the pilot's asm10 window alignment simply failed to align it), present_elsewhere, absent (covered < 20%), repetitive (>= 5 landings), N_run.

3. Validation of curate.py. A synthetic two-haplotype genome (4 real query genes with introns; in hap2 a 1-bp deletion in exon 2 of KLRD1, a deleted LILRB2 locus, a 6 kb insertion) was run through the full pipeline and the curation: the frameshift was located to the right exon, the deleted locus was classed absent between aligned flanks, the insertion absent from hap1, duplicates across windows were collapsed.

5. Loci that vanish on re-alignment. 9 (pair 1), 4 (pair 2) and 7 (pair 3) pilot loci return no hit of their family when their +-5 kb region is searched together with all other regions and all 99 queries (column curation_note = no_hit_of_this_family_on_realignment). As a diagnostic, each such region was searched alone with its evidence query alone: the hit is then found in every case except two MHC-I partial loci of pair 3 (curation_note ...alone_with_evidence_query:found(cov, fs, st, ident, ms)). These are therefore weak hits (identity 0.36-0.66, scores 279-1258) that fall under miniprot's relative-score output filter (--outs 0.3 / -p 0.3) as soon as a stronger copy of the same query is present in the same run; in the pilot the filter was applied per 250 Mb chunk and then re-applied globally on the merged output, which let them through. They are real alignments but sit at the detection threshold; most are partial or pseudogene-like, one is a pilot "intact" LILR locus (pair 1 hap1_L078: alone, cov 0.80, 0 frameshifts).

4. Limitations of the curation pass. Still no transcript or read-level evidence: a frameshift in a homopolymer may be a sequencing/assembly error, a near-identical extra copy in one haplotype may be an assembly duplication or a collapsed repeat in the other, and start codons are judged only where the human/mouse/Rousettus query aligns from residue 1 (a bat protein with a different N-terminus is reported as not_aligned or non-ATG, not as a pseudogene). Back-search uses Swiss-Prot human+mouse only; bat-specific expansions can only be labelled by their closest human/mouse relative. Region re-alignment is competition-free (no other genomic copies to absorb secondary alignments), so status on the +-5 kb region can legitimately differ from the whole-genome call in tandem arrays (LILR, CD1, MHC-I).

READ-LEVEL AND TRANSCRIPT-LEVEL CHECKS, 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-15 (scripts verify_driver.sh, verify_driver2.sh, hifi_stream.sh, pstream.py, get_rna.sh, fetch_windows.py, refseq_start.py, hifi_check.py, rna_check.py, readcheck_lib.py; outputs in verify/<pair>/; journal logs/verify.log)

6. Reference used for all read mapping. The pilot windows (+-100 kb around each gene cluster, exactly as compare_regions.py extracted them) of both haplotypes were fetched from NCBI nuccore (fetch_windows.py -> verify/<pair>/windows_hap1.fa, windows_hap2.fa, windows.tsv; pair 3: 22 windows, 15.4 Mb for both haplotypes). Reads were mapped to these windows, not to the whole genomes (the genomes were no longer on disk). Consequence: reads from paralogous copies elsewhere in the genome can land in the windows; the MAPQ and divergence filters below remove most but not all of them, which is why only the MAPQ >= 20 counts are used for conclusions about copies.

6a. HiFi reads of the assembled individual (same BioSample as the assembly). Runs: pair 3 SRR39556278 (mEidDup1, Revio, 72 Gbp in total), pair 1 SRR38296513 (mMyoSod1, Revio, 120 Gbp), pair 2 SRR36951662 (mRhiCli1, Sequel II, 36 Gbp). A prefix of each fastq.gz was streamed from ENA (pstream.py, 8 parallel HTTPS range requests, 32 MB chunks) through gzip -dc | minimap2 -x map-hifi -c --cs --secondary=no -t 3 against both haplotypes' windows as one reference; reads were never written to disk, the truncated last record is dropped. The prefix is a random-enough sample of the run (reads are not sorted by position). Pair 3: planned 15 GB, cut by hand at 11.27 GB (06:40 on 2026-11-13, laptop had to be switched off) -> 386,399 PAF records, 8,346 kept after filters (primary, aligned block >= 2 kb, divergence de <= 0.02), median depth 4-9x per window (hifi_depth.tsv). Pair 1: the full planned 18 GB (2026-11-15 02:25-13:31, 0.3-0.45 MB/s from ENA) -> 2,572,740 PAF records, 46,477 kept, median depth 5-11x per window (one window 19x, one 3x). Pair 2: the full planned 20 GB (2026-11-15 13:31-17:29, 1.4 MB/s) -> 705,323 PAF records, 10,492 kept, median depth 3-7x per window (Sequel II yields about 1.1 Gbp per GB of fastq.gz against 2.3-2.7 for Revio, hence the lower depth). All three pairs done 2026-11-15 17:29 (VERIFY_DONE).

6b. hifi_check.py. Depth of every locus, hap-only locus and >= 5 kb gap is expressed relative to the median depth of its window (ratio = locus depth / window median; MAPQ >= 20 variant also reported). Verdicts: window median < 4 -> low_coverage; ratio >= 1.7 -> about_2x (the sequence is present in both haplotypes, so the other assembly lacks a copy); 0.55-1.7 -> about_1x, supported as assembled (flagged when no MAPQ >= 20 read spans the locus); 0.2-0.55 -> below_1x, reads split over copies (possible assembly duplication). A >= 5 kb deletion is called real in the alternative haplotype when its segment has ratio < 1.5 and >= 2 clean spanning reads; a segment at >= 1.7 is present in both haplotypes. Variant sites: for every status_differs_confirmed difference with variants in the CDS of the better copy, the variant position is lifted to the alternative copy (minimap2 -x asm5 --cs of the two loci), and reads are counted at both copies within +-2 bp (substitutions) or +-(homopolymer length + 2) bp (indels). The variant is "supported by reads of the alternative haplotype" if >= 3 reads cover the alternative site and at most max(1, 20%) of them carry the better copy's allele; "NOT in reads: assembly error" if >= 2 reads and >= 60% carry the better allele; otherwise "mixed (heterozygous or collapsed copies)". Fewer than 3 reads -> low_coverage_at_alt.

6b.1. Depth figures. Fig. 3a plots ratio_mapq20 for the 472 loci with window median >=4 and uses the exploratory flags in depth_null_fdr.tsv (nominal BH q <0.05). Zero ratios were assigned z = -8. The resulting screen is uncalibrated. The denominator of ratio_mapq20 is the original all-primary-read window median. The fixed corridor on this column is 0.55 <= ratio < 1.7. The 23 zero-depth loci across all 476 measured loci were checked against the original PAFs using the same target interval, padding and filters as hifi_check.py. Twenty enter Fig. 3a. Fig. 3b reads the family-level sums from copies_implied_vs_assembled.tsv, with window median >=4 applied per locus before grouping. The plotted count and sum use the same eligible loci. Absolute differences >2 are highlighted. These sums are diagnostics and may be dominated by local pile-ups. For the hap1_L008 audit, we retained primary PAF records with block >=2 kb, divergence <=0.02 and MAPQ >=20. We reconstructed reference-position coverage from the cs tag, counting matched and substituted bases and excluding target deletions. The CDS intervals come from the best pilot HLA-B-query GFF hit (hap1c01_000010). See manuscript/derived/rclivosus_hap1_L008_depth.tsv.

6c. RNA-seq. No RNA exists for the assembled individuals; related animals were used, so allele-level statements are limited to loci that are not polymorphic between individuals (see 6d). Runs (ENA, prefix per mate in parentheses): pair 1 SRR1657060, Myotis myotis, blood (2 x 1.2 GB); pair 2 SRR13417526, Rhinolophus clivosus, heart (SAMN17088689; NextSeq 500, whole run, 2 x 1.1 GB) and SRR36602444, Rhinolophus ferrumequinum, spleen (NovaSeq X, 2 x 1.0 GB); pair 3 SRR27278001, Eidolon helvum, blood (HiSeq X, 2 x 1.2 GB). Mates were mapped as single-end reads with minimap2 -x splice:sr -c --cs --secondary=no -t 2 against each haplotype's windows separately (rna_check.py --map). Filters: primary, aligned block >= 50 bp, de <= 0.12 (cross-species reads), and, since 2026-11-15, only reads within 5 kb of a pilot locus (the first pair-1 run was killed by the kernel at 24 million records: 97% of the blood reads of SRR1657060 map to the first 50 kb of window hap1_MHC-I_1, a chromosome end 50 kb away from the nearest locus, and are irrelevant to every count below). Per locus: reads, spliced reads, junctions seen >= 2 times; at the model start: reads over the first 60 bp, reads in the 100 bp upstream of the model, and junction reads whose acceptor lies at the model start and whose donor is upstream. Verdicts: < 3 reads -> not_expressed_or_not_covered; >= 2 such junction reads -> upstream_exon_spliced_into_model_start (the real start is upstream, the model's first exon is truncated); >= 3 reads both over the start and upstream -> transcript_extends_upstream_of_model_start; >= 3 over the start only -> transcript_starts_near_model_start. "Expressed" in the summary means >= 3 reads anywhere in the locus. Variant sites: counts at both copies as in 6b (MAPQ >= 1), reported as the number of transcripts carrying the disruptive versus the intact allele.

6c.1. KLRD1 junction sensitivity to mapping quality (pair3). We reran the original rna_check.py without --map on the saved PAFs in a separate verification directory, using the same windows.tsv and window FASTAs. A second run used the same files with PAF column 12 filtered to >=20 before loading. Both runs used the same intron parsing and upstream-junction rule. We joined KLRD1 rows by locus_id and recorded rna_reads and junction_reads_into_model_start from each run in manuscript/derived/klrd1_junction_mapq.tsv. The unfiltered per-locus and variant-site TSVs matched the archived tables byte for byte. The raw PAF counts matched as well. The total kept before locus overlap differed in the historical summary (current proximity filter: 348,212 and 367,363 for hap1 and hap2; historical summary: 608,053 and 610,985). For cross-haplotype sharing, we counted distinct (read ID, query length) pairs among the original retained records whose target span overlapped at least one KLRD1 model, using the same overlap rule as rna_check.py. Counts in hap1, hap2 and their intersection were 456, 454 and 395, respectively. Separate references and shared read IDs limit per-copy attribution.

6d. Start codons with bat RefSeq proteins (refseq_start.py). For every pilot-intact locus (+-5 kb), the RefSeq proteins of the same family from an annotated relative were aligned with miniprot and the best hit's query start and first codon recorded: Myotis myotis (120 proteins) for pair 1, Rhinolophus ferrumequinum (109) for pair 2, Rousettus aegyptiacus (97) for pair 3 (verify/<pair>/tmp/relative_queries.faa; accessions in refseq_start.tsv). A locus whose human/mouse query did not align from residue 1 but whose bat query does, with ATG, has a bat-type N-terminus, not a defect (ATG_from_residue_1). non_ATG means the bat protein also starts at a non-ATG codon at that locus (with the nearest in-frame ATG reported); this is the case for the KLRD1 arrays of pair 3 (13 loci, TCG/TCT) and for single KLRD1 loci of pairs 1 and 2 (TCT/TCG), which the RNA of pair 2 resolves as transcripts starting upstream of the model (6c).

6e. Limitations. (i) Mapping to windows, not genomes (6). (ii) HiFi depth 6-7x for pair 3 after the cut: a locus with 3-5 reads at a site is borderline, and the about_2x / below_1x calls (ratio thresholds) are made on medians of a few reads per locus; they are candidates, not copy-number calls. (iii) RNA from other individuals and, for pairs 2 and 3, partly from other species: expression and splice structure transfer, alleles do not (MHC-I). (iv) Prefixes, not whole runs: for RNA this only lowers depth; for HiFi the prefix is unsorted and acts as a subsample. (v) Blood, spleen and heart; a locus "not expressed" here may be expressed elsewhere.
